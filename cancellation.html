Cancellation & Refund Terms

Last updated: July 9, 2026 | Version 1.1

1. Cancellation Overview

At Transportizy, we provide flexible cancellation options for all our transport divisions. Cancellations can be requested by logging in and navigating to your travel profile, or contacting our assistance desk.

2. Railway Cancellations

Refund values depend on when the request is logged relative to the departure schedule:

  • More than 48 hours: 90% refund of the total ticket cost.
  • Between 24 and 48 hours: 75% refund of the total ticket cost.
  • Between 12 and 24 hours: 50% refund of the total ticket cost.
  • Less than 12 hours: Ticket is locked, and no refund is available.

3. Airways Cancellations

Flight cancellation rules vary based on specific airline partner terms:

  • More than 24 hours before flight: Base fare is refundable minus standard agency service charges.
  • Less than 24 hours before flight: Flights are considered non-refundable, except for terminal and local government taxes.

4. Roadways Cancellations

Bus ticket refund terms are as follows:

  • More than 12 hours before coach departure: 85% refund of the ticket cost.
  • Less than 12 hours before coach departure: No refund is processed.

5. Cargo Shipping Cancellations

Cargo dispatch orders can be cancelled with a 100% refund prior to pickup or dispatch departure. Once physical parcels have been collected by our logistics agents and entered the sorting networks, cancellation is disabled.

6. Refund Processing

Valid refunds are credited directly back to your original source of payment within 3-5 business days. For mock tickets booked under test scenarios, simulation values are instantly re-credited to your local storage account balance.
