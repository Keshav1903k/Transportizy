Our Story

Connecting People & Businesses Globally

Established in 2016, Transportizy was born out of a simple vision: to make transit booking stress-free. We realized that travelers had to visit separate, outdated sites to buy train, flight, or bus tickets, and companies struggled to schedule cargo deliveries. We brought them all together on a single high-performance engine.

Today, we serve millions of customers, partnering with state railways, leading airlines, premium bus coach companies, and logistics warehouses to make travels swift, safe, and transparent.

Core Values

What Drives Transportizy

Our foundational principles guide how we build our software, serve our travelers, and handle worldwide cargo deliveries daily.

Uncompromised Safety

We enforce strict verification checks across all partner rail, flight, and coach networks, prioritizing the safety and security of your passengers and cargo above all else.

Reliability & Speed

Our high-performance booking engines process ticketing routes instantly, ensuring you get on-time alerts, prompt customer support, and instant reschedules.

Green Operations

We are dedicated to sustainable transit. We offset local carbon emissions by backing eco-friendly rail routes and integrating carbon calculation reports for commercial cargo fleets.
