Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026 | Version 1.1

1. Introduction

At Transportizy, we are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and disclose information from users of our multi-modal booking platform, which includes train, flight, bus, and cargo logistics services.

2. Information We Collect

We gather various types of information to process your bookings and deliver high-quality services:

  • Personal Identification Data: Full name, email address, phone number, and account credentials when registering or completing a checkout.
  • Travel & Routing Choices: Search queries, station coordinates, departure times, passenger counts, selected transit class, and cargo parcel details (weight, packaging type).
  • Technical Usage Information: Local storage states, session tokens, browser specifications, and IP locations to prevent fraudulent activities.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed based on legitimate commercial needs and legal mandates:

  • To generate valid e-tickets and dispatch reservation details to your email.
  • To verify account authentications (sign-up, sign-in) and preserve your booking history in local databases.
  • To compute optimal routing tariffs and dynamically adjust class-specific pricing.
  • To communicate support updates and respond to inquiries sent via our contact panel.

4. Data Storage and Security

We deploy modern encryption standards (TLS/SSL) for transiting web requests. Account settings and local reservation records are maintained inside secure local storage namespaces. No sensitive credit card numbers or financial passwords are saved on our infrastructure.

5. Sharing of Personal Data

We coordinate only with authorized transport operators (rail networks, commercial airlines, local coach companies, and cargo dispatch couriers) to validate your travel credentials. We will never sell, lease, or rent your database records to marketing networks.

6. Your Legal Rights

You maintain rights regarding the data we hold, including: requesting copies of your user profiles, modifying records that are outdated, and clearing local database cache items by logging out of the profile settings.

7. Contact Support

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or seek data erasure, please connect with our team on the Support Contact page.
