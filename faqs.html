Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our booking procedures, baggage allowances, cancellation terms, and cargo dispatch details.

To reschedule your travel date, log in to your account, head to "My Bookings" in your profile menu, select the active ticket, and click "Reschedule". Rescheduling can be done up to 24 hours prior to departure, subject to a minor rescheduling fee and carrier availability.
Standard baggage allowances vary by flight class. Economy tickets allow 1 cabin bag (up to 7kg) and 1 check-in bag (up to 20kg). Business and First Class bookings allow up to 2 check-in bags (32kg maximum combined). Extra weight allowances can be purchased during checkout on our booking page.
Once your cancellation request is processed, refunds are immediately dispatched. Depending on your financial institution, the refund will reflect in your bank account or card statement within 5 to 7 business days. A cancellation fee may be deducted depending on how close to departure you cancelled.
Yes, door-to-door pickup and delivery are fully supported for all standard cargo and document packages. When filling out the cargo booking form, simply choose the "Express Courier Door-to-Door" dispatch option, and our logistics agent will arrive at your address for package pickup.
Absolutely. Transportizy utilizes AES-256 standard encryption algorithms to secure all data transmissions. User credentials are encrypted at storage level, and credit/debit card transactions are processed directly by global PCI-DSS certified payment gateways without ever touch-logging on our database.
In case of delay or transit schedule changes, Transportizy will dispatch automated alerts via registered SMS and email immediately. For delays exceeding 3 hours, customers are eligible for free alternative arrangements or a full refund, depending on the operator's service level agreement.
